MEASUREMENT NOTES

What is measured?

  • Approximate left/right landmark alignment
  • Vertical forehead–midface–lower-face thirds
  • Eye distance relative to eye width
  • Face height-to-width proportion

These are 2D image estimates and change with head angle, lens distortion, expression, lighting and landmark detection quality.

Important context

There is no universal scientific attractiveness score.

Facial aesthetics research studies population-level preferences and specific perceptual cues; it does not establish a single objective score for an individual. This app deliberately avoids ranking a person's worth, identity, ethnicity or gender. Its index summarizes only a few measurable image geometry properties, not attractiveness, health or model-industry potential.
